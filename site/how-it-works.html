How it works

A route, from sender to receiver

A resident activates the courier, records or writes their message, and watches it leave. The robot signals its state the entire time, no guessing required.

The robot

TurtleBot3, outfitted for delivery

A TurtleBot3 Burger with a custom mail compartment, RFID reader, LED indicator, and speaker for arrival signals.

Message types

Two ways to say something

Users pick whatever feels natural: a handwritten note or a recorded voice message.

✉

Physical note

Write by hand, place it in the compartment, and watch the robot carry it down the hall. This feature is paired with voice messages for residents with dexterity limits.

🎙

Voice postcard

Record a brief, confidential audio message. Skip the blank-page pressure, and just speak and send.

Pain points
  • Uncertainty about whether it's a good time to reach the recipient
  • May not know how to select someone if the interface isn't intuitive
  • Physical difficulty writing (arthritis, tremor)
  • Blank page anxiety
  • Uncertainty about tone or what's appropriate to share
  • Unclear confirmation that loading succeeded
  • Physical difficulty opening/closing compartment
  • Dependency on staff if limited mobility
  • No visibility into delivery status or timing
  • Anxiety builds with silence
  • Confusion if the arrival signal is unclear
  • Missed delivery if resident is asleep, out, or doesn't notice the robot (hard of hearing, low vision)
  • Pressure or obligation to reciprocate
  • Uncertainty about etiquette (how soon to reply, whether silence is rude)
  • Risk of one-sided exchange if unanswered

Design feedback

What we learned, and what changed

Peer feedback

Feedback came from instructors and other students throughout the PBL program. Given the project timeline, our next step would be to conduct direct resident interviews.

Outcomes

Suggestions and questions led us to consider edge cases such as what happens if the recipient isn't present, what stops unauthorized access to the compartment, or how to handle unsolicited deliveries.
