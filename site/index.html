6. Q&A (3 Minutes)

Anticipated Questions & Discussion Points

Key technical, operational, and clinical considerations for the 3-minute question period.

Q1

How does the robot ensure senior safety in narrow shared hallways?

Speed-capped at 0.22 m/s with 360° LiDAR maintaining a 0.3m safety margin and proximity auto-stop.
Q2

Why physical letters and voice notes instead of regular tablet video calls?

Asynchronous notes remove scheduling pressure and provide lasting tangible keepsakes that seniors cherish.
Q3

How is privacy protected for audio postcards and topic suggestions?

Audio is stored locally with pre-recorded .wav prompts, ensuring zero patient data leaks to external cloud services.
Q4

How does the robot handle multi-story facilities or navigating elevators?

The current prototype operates strictly on a single-floor layout. Scaling to multi-story facilities would require elevator API integration or staff helper checkpoints.
