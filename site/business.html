Sizing the opportunity: US vs. Japan

For anyone who wants the market context.

Product landscape

Individual pieces of our solution exist with no direct competitor combining them:

  • Robots that run group activities (e.g., trivia sessions)
  • Companion robots in pet-like roles
  • Robots that write letters for businesses, with AI-assisted composition
  • Caregiving dynamics between staff and residents, not resident to resident

Market viability

  • Extensive documentation of the social isolation problem in elder care
  • Facilities already spend on companion/engagement robots
  • Real appetite for "snail mail" and human-composed creations over app notifications

Market sizing

USJapan
TAMAll facilities × ~$18K/facility ~30K facilities → ~$540M ~70K facilities → ~$1.26B
SAMTech-forward, privately paid ~15–20% → 5.5K facilities → ~$99M ~25–30% → ~20K facilities → ~$360M
SOMRealistic early pilot 50 facilities → ~$900K 75–100 facilities → ~$1.4–1.8M

Japan's TAM/SAM run larger on sheer facility count and cultural openness to social robotics, but purchasing often routes through public long-term-care insurance instead of discretionary spend as in the U.S. This a real barrier the raw market size doesn't capture.
