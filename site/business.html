Current Competitive Landscape

Existing solutions target different touchpoints:

  • Group Activity Robots: Lead trivia, but lack 1-on-1 peer bonding. [1]
  • Companion Pet Robots: Comfort from an object, not human connection. [2]
  • Commercial Scribes: B2B enterprise letters, not eldercare. [3]
  • Staff-to-Resident Tools: Medical workflows, missing neighbor joy. (team analysis, not independently sourced)

Market Viability & Demand Validation

  • Staff Efficiency: Saves manual transit hours for activity staff.
  • Nostalgic Resonance: Real physical artifacts beat sterile app alerts. [3] (general consumer signal; not resident-specific)
  • Existing Budgets: Facilities already budget for resident engagement tech. (inferred from existing deployments, see [1][2])

Market Sizing

TAM, SAM, and SOM: US vs Japan Analysis

A realistic comparison of facility addressability and pilot rollout milestones.

Market Tier United States (US) Japan (JP)
TAM (Total Addressable) All facilities × ~$18K/facility ~30,000 facilities [6][7] → ~$540 Million ~70,000 facilities [8] → ~$1.26 Billion
SAM (Serviceable Addressable) Tech-forward private-pay facilities ~15–20% → ~5,500 facilities → ~$99 Million ~25–30% → ~20,000 facilities → ~$360 Million
SOM (Serviceable Obtainable) Realistic early pilot rollout 50 pilot facilities → ~$900,000 75–100 pilot facilities → ~$1.4–1.8 Million

Team estimates: Per-facility cost (~$18K), SAM capture percentages (15–20% US, 25–30% Japan), and SOM projections (50 facilities US, 75–100 Japan) are our team's own estimates based on comparable robot deployment patterns, not drawn from published pricing or market-penetration data.

  1. US assisted living facility count: Forbes Health — approximately 30,600 communities nationally.
  2. US assisted living facility count (cross-reference): Grand View Research, citing AHCA data — 32,231 licensed communities.
  3. Japan facility count (~70,000) derived by aggregating multiple official care facility categories from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) Annual Survey of Long-term Care Service Facilities and Establishments. Japan does not have a single "assisted living" category equivalent to the US. The four largest surveyed categories alone total approximately 46,097: special nursing homes (Tokuyo: 8,621), healthcare facilities for the elderly (Roken: 4,214), community-based day services (18,921), and dementia group homes (14,341). The ~70,000 figure includes these four categories plus additional in-home and community service types from the same survey, aggregated by the team. Source: MHLW 2024 Annual Survey of Long-term Care Services (厚生労働省・令和6年介護サービス施設・事業所調査).
